## Why International Patients Choose India

India has become the global hub for hair transplants, competing directly with Turkey. While Turkey is famous for its marketing, India is famous for its **medical expertise**. Most top Indian surgeons are ABHRS (American Board) certified.

### The Cost Comparison

| Country | Avg Cost (2500 Grafts) | Price Per Graft |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **USA / UK** | $15,000 - $25,000 | $6.00 - $10.00 |
| **Turkey** | $2,500 - $4,000 | $1.00 - $1.50 |
| **India** | **$1,500 - $3,000** | **$0.60 - $1.20** |

### What affects the price?

1.  **Technique:** DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) costs slightly more than FUE.
2.  **Surgeon vs Technician:** In India, you can choose packages where the **doctor** performs the surgery, not just technicians.
3.  **City:** Clinics in Chennai and Bangalore often offer better rates than Mumbai or Delhi for the same quality.
